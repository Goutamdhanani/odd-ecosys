## What S00 can claim from this trace

The selected source has a connected set of research, contact, qualification, verification, campaign, and reply modules, plus public routes that expose several of those transitions. Existing tests demonstrate important slices with temporary databases and fake or loopback transports. This evidence supports partial capability mapping; it does not establish a composed research-to-revenue loop or full S00 acceptance.

The observed system outcomes stop at persisted lead/contact data, check evidence, campaign/send state, SMTP acceptance, and possible inbound reply attribution. A final business outcome such as a booked meeting, paid order, realized revenue, or experiment-level causal attribution was not found in this trace. The selected source does not provide a canonical ledger linking a research run through a final business outcome. Record those as unknown until an actual model, API, or data contract and its tests establish them.

## Safety and execution limits

The source routes and modules have live side-effect boundaries at provider search, website HTTP, DNS/MX and optional SMTP verification, outbound SMTP, inbound IMAP, and external CRM sync. This trace used static source inspection plus the isolated lifecycle fixture only. That fixture ran under the repository’s normal `NODE_ENV=test` harness (which enables its built-in test auth bypass unless `ODD_AUTH_ENFORCE_TESTS=true`) and used only disposable SQLite data and fixture SMTP credentials on loopback. The trace did not invoke external provider, DNS, mailbox, CRM, or production auth paths, query a live database, or claim complete end-to-end execution.
